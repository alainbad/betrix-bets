Welcome to the harbour.

Ten fixed paylines pay left to right from the first reel. Three or more matching symbols win. The angler is wild and substitutes for paying symbols. Cash bass and compass scatters do not pay on lines.

The angler's haul

Three or more compasses anywhere on a paid spin award 10 free spins. Cash bass show values of 1×, 2×, 3×, 5×, 10× or 20× the total bet. During free spins, each angler collects the sum of all cash fish, multiplied by the current collection level. Fish pay only when collected.

Every four anglers advance the collection level: 1× → 2× → 3× → 5×, awarding 10 extra spins at each advance. The new level applies from the next spin. At 5× no further spins are awarded. Compass scatters do not retrigger during the feature. Each free spin is started by tapping Spin; the triggering bet stays locked.

The free-spin preview uses a separate demo balance. End it with the preview button. Browser cryptographic randomness selects independent symbols with weights 14, 10, 12, 14, 16, 12, 4, 3, 18 in symbol order. Cash values are drawn equally from [1,2,2,3,5,10,20].
