Multiplier spotlight

Before the wheel turns, one of the six numbers is chosen equally for a boost of 2×, 3× or 5×, also equally likely. If that number wins, its profit odds are multiplied by the boost. The winning stake is returned once; losing bets are lost.

Example: a 50-credit bet on 10 returns 550 credits normally. If 10 has a 3× spotlight, it returns 1,550 credits: 1,500 profit plus the 50-credit winning stake.

Demo details

The result and spotlight are independently drawn with browser cryptographic randomness. The wheel animation displays that result; timing clicks cannot change it. Maximum total bet is 100,000 credits. This original experimental game has no certified RTP, no real dealer stream and no cash value. Refresh starts a new session.
