MIDNIGHT VAULT

The inside information.

10 fixed paylines. Wins run left to right from reel one, with 3 or more matching symbols. Only the highest win on each line pays. Your total bet is split equally across 10 lines.

Master keys & vault locks

A master key expands its entire reel into wilds before paylines are evaluated. Wilds replace all paying symbols. Three or more vault locks on the original grid trigger Crack the Safe, even when a key expands over a lock.

In the bonus, pick 3 of 6 safes. Each contains a unique shuffled prize: 2×, 3×, 5×, 8×, 12× or 20× your total bet. These prizes are added to any line wins.

Demo details

Each position is independently drawn with weights: diamond 18, gold 16, crown 14, emerald 14, ruby 12, watch 10, key 1, lock 3, coin 10 (98 total). Outcomes use browser cryptographic randomness. This is an experimental game, not a certified wagering product. Refreshing restarts the session.
