Meet your winning pack.

Each paid spin has six reels with 2–7 symbols each, creating 64–117,649 ways. Three or more matching symbols on adjacent reels, starting from the leftmost reel, pay in any row. There are no fixed paylines. Each symbol pays for its longest consecutive match only.

Doghouse wilds

Wilds appear on reels 2–5 and substitute for paying symbols. Each has a 2× or 3× multiplier. Multipliers multiply together within an individual way; all winning ways are added. Golden paw scatters do not substitute or pay on ways.

Home sweet home

Three or more golden paw scatters on a paid spin award 8 free spins at the triggering bet. During free spins all reels have seven positions, creating 117,649 ways. Every doghouse wild locks in its position with its multiplier until the feature ends. Scatters do not retrigger. Tap Free for each spin.

A spin pays at most 5,000× total bet. Each paying symbol has weight 16, scatter weight 2, and wild weight 1 on the four middle reels (zero on the outer reels). Wild multipliers are equally likely 2× or 3×. Browser cryptographic randomness is used. This original experimental demo has no certified RTP and no cash value. Refresh resets your session.
