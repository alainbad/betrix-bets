Blackjack Royale

Beat the dealer without going over 21. Aces count as 1 or 11; face cards count as 10. A two-card natural blackjack pays 3:2 profit, plus your stake. Other wins pay 1:1 and ties return your stake.

The dealer checks for blackjack before you act and stands on all 17s, including soft 17. Hit takes a card. Stand ends the hand. Double adds an equal stake, gives one card and ends the hand.

Identical ranks may split once into two hands if you can fund the additional stake. Doubling after splitting is allowed. Split aces receive one card each and stand. A 21 after splitting pays 1:1, not blackjack odds. No insurance, surrender or resplitting. Use H / S / D / P shortcuts when a hand is active.

A six-deck shoe is shuffled when fewer than 52 cards remain before a round. Cards are drawn without replacement. Reset and refresh start a new demo session.

Shuffles and bonus draws use browser cryptographic randomness. This experimental virtual-credit game has no certified RTP and no cash value. It is not connected to an account wallet or a live dealer stream.
