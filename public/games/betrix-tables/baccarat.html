Mega Baccarat

Bet on Player, Banker or Tie. Each hand’s value is the last digit of its card sum: aces are 1, tens and faces are 0. Closest to 9 wins. Drawing is automatic under standard punto banco rules.

An initial 8 or 9 is a natural: both hands stand. Otherwise Player draws on 0–5 and stands on 6–7. If Player stands, Banker draws on 0–5. If Player draws, Banker draws on 0–2; on 3 except against an 8; on 4 against 2–7; on 5 against 4–7; and on 6 against 6–7.

Player wins pay 1:1 profit. Banker wins pay 0.95:1 profit after 5% commission. Tie wins pay 8:1 profit. On a tie, Player and Banker stakes are returned. Winning stakes are returned once in addition to profit.

Mega rounds

After bets close, 1 in 8 rounds gets a profit multiplier chosen equally from 2×, 3× or 5×. The multiplier applies to the winning betting area’s net profit only; returned stakes and pushes are not multiplied. Example: 100 credits on Banker with a 3× boost returns 385 credits (285 profit + 100 stake). This is an original demo variation.

An eight-deck shoe is shuffled when fewer than 52 cards remain. Cards are drawn without replacement. No side bets. Reset and refresh start a new session.

Shuffles and bonus draws use browser cryptographic randomness. This experimental virtual-credit game has no certified RTP and no cash value. It is not connected to an account wallet or a live dealer stream.
