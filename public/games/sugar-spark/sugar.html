Make a little magic.

Five or more matching candies connected horizontally or vertically form a winning cluster. Diagonal neighbours do not connect. Winning candies pop and new candies tumble down.

Spark spots

The first hit lights a spot. The second hit gives it 2×, then 4×, 8×, up to 128×. A cluster uses the sum of its active spot multipliers, or 1× if none are active. Multipliers apply before the current hit upgrades a spot.

Cluster payouts

All seven candies pay the same: 5 candies = 0.3× bet; 6–7 = 0.6×; 8–9 = 1.5×; 10–11 = 3×; 12–14 = 6×; 15+ = 12×, before spot multipliers.

Spark party

Four golden lollipops on the initial paid board award 10 free spins at the triggering bet. Spots persist through the feature. No retriggers. Tap Spin for each free spin. Trying the feature uses a separate demo balance; end it with the same button.

Each spin stops at 20 winning tumbles or 5,000× bet. Random symbols use browser cryptographic randomness; weights are 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18 for candies and 2 for the scatter. This experimental game has no certified RTP. Refresh resets the session.
